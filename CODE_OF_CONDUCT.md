# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of factors like age, disability, ethnicity, gender, or sexual orientation. We aim to foster an open, welcoming, and healthy community.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect for differing viewpoints. We also prioritize constructive criticism and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, and the sharing of private information.

## Enforcement
Community leaders will enforce these standards and take appropriate action against inappropriate or harmful conduct.

## Reporting
Violations can be reported to the project maintainers via **[Email](skalidara@gmail.com)**.
